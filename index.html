About the Workshop

Who we are

This is the third workshop in the series "Black Heroes of Statistical Research", dedicated to highlighting achievements made by researchers in Africa in spite of all the odds.

The workshop brings together researchers, students, and practitioners to share knowledge, build networks, and celebrate the contributions of Black statisticians and mathematical scientists.

Date: 18–19 December 2026

Format: Online via Zoom

zoom.us/my/blackheroes

Motivation

Why this workshop matters

"Only about 1% of the world's research originates from African scholars based in Africa — not due to a lack of talent, but insufficient support and resources."

African researchers often struggle for recognition in projects to which they have significantly contributed, particularly in data collection and processing. To address these issues, the charity Educate Africa was founded in 2017, empowering African scholars to tackle problems critical to their countries and regions, with active collaborations across more than 30 African countries.

Workshop aims

  1. Present talks by African researchers based in Africa, highlighting their groundbreaking work.
  2. Showcase their achievements and outline their future aspirations.
  3. Inspire researchers in the UK and beyond to engage in meaningful collaborations with African scholars.

This initiative aims to create a global network of support and recognition, enabling African researchers to gain the visibility and resources they deserve while addressing pressing local and global challenges.
